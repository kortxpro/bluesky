Careers

Work where the standard is the product.

We hire people who want their work measured. Supervisors, technicians, account managers, and recovery specialists, trained to written standards, reviewed on written criteria, and retained because retention is a KPI.

Why BlueSky

Three reasons the team stays.

Commercial cleaning has a turnover problem. We have written policies against each of the reasons turnover happens, because we measure retention as a number.

Training is paid.

Onboarding is paid, not on-your-own-time. Product training, chemistry training, equipment training, safety and PPE, all paid hours, documented, and re-certified on schedule.

Expectations are written.

Your scope is documented. Your inspection criteria are documented. Your escalation path is documented. You never find out what the job was after you failed it.

Performance is reviewed.

Reviews happen on a schedule, not when someone remembers. Your inspection scores, attendance, and client feedback are reviewed with you, not behind you.

Benefits

Standard practice, not benefits marketing.

What we actually provide, not aspirational list items.

Paid training

All onboarding, safety, and product training delivered as paid hours with documented completion.

Written standards

Scope, frequency, and inspection criteria provided in writing before your first shift.

Scheduled reviews

Quarterly performance conversations with your supervisor, using the same numbers the client sees.

Emergency pay

Disaster-recovery mobilization pays differently from standard shifts. Mobilization pay, overtime, and meal allowances are documented.

Equipment & PPE

Provided by BlueSky. You do not buy your own gloves, masks, or cleaning supplies.

Growth paths

Technician → lead → supervisor → account manager paths are written, not discovered. If you want to move up, the criteria are published.
