What We Do

Seven programs. One discipline.

From recurring janitorial to end-stage disaster recovery, specialized teams, documented scopes, measured results. Each service is available on its own or integrated into a single facility contract.

Programs

Seven programs, each with its own scope, process, and industries served.

01 · Janitorial

Recurring commercial cleaning programs.

Nightly, daily, or custom-frequency janitorial tailored to occupancy, traffic, and compliance context. Written scopes. Trained crews. Supervisor walk-throughs every shift.

  • General office, common-area, and amenity cleaning
  • Restroom disinfection and consumable management
  • Trash consolidation and waste-stream separation
  • Periodic detail cleans on written frequency
  • Monthly inspection scores & performance brief
Explore Janitorial →
02 · Pressure Washing

Exterior surfaces, restored on schedule.

Building envelopes, parking structures, sidewalks, dumpster pads, and drive-throughs restored with calibrated high-pressure systems, surface-appropriate PSI, degreasers, and recovery where required.

  • Facade and building-envelope soft wash
  • Concrete, pavers, and sidewalk deep clean
  • Parking structure degreasing
  • Drive-through and dumpster pad maintenance
  • Graffiti and gum removal
Explore Pressure Washing →
03 · Window Cleaning

Interior, exterior, and high-rise glass.

From ground-level storefronts to full-height curtain walls. Waterfed poles, rope access, and lift-based methods, chosen by the building, not the vendor. Safety program documented per OSHA guidelines.

  • Interior storefront and partition glass
  • Exterior up to six stories, waterfed pole
  • High-rise facades, rope access / boom lift
  • Post-construction glass & sealant removal
  • Scheduled quarterly or monthly programs
Explore Window Cleaning →
04 · Carpet Cleaning

Hot-water extraction & restoration.

Commercial carpet programs, quarterly maintenance through full restoration, including water-damage recovery. Low-moisture encapsulation for same-day turnover, full extraction for deep reset.

  • Hot-water extraction (deep clean)
  • Low-moisture encapsulation (fast turnover)
  • Spot and stain treatment
  • Water-damage extraction & drying
  • Carpet replacement coordination (if needed)
Explore Carpet Cleaning →
05 · Floor Stripping & Waxing

Hard-surface restoration, on occupied schedules.

VCT, terrazzo, concrete, and LVT restoration, strip, scrub, seal, and finish, scheduled around tenant occupancy, not through it. Documented process, sealer specs, and dry times.

  • VCT strip & wax (multi-coat)
  • Terrazzo polish & grind
  • Concrete densification & seal
  • LVT deep clean & protective coat
  • Burnishing on rotating schedule
Explore Floor Stripping & Waxing →
06 · Post-Construction & Disaster Recovery

Turnover cleans. Water events. Full recovery.

The work Hurricane Milton proved we can do. Rough, final, and turnover cleans for construction. Debris removal, water extraction, industrial dehumidification, and full interior restoration for disaster events.

  • Rough, final, and punch-list cleans
  • Debris removal and staging
  • Emergency water extraction
  • Industrial dehumidification
  • Carpet / hard-surface / envelope restoration
  • Documentation for insurance / ownership
Explore Post-Construction → Hurricane Milton case →
07 · Property Decommission & Fog Disinfect

Closing buildings safely. Opening them cleanly.

Full-property decommission and electrostatic fog disinfection for vacated, transitioning, or post-event commercial spaces. Documented protocols. EPA-registered chemistry. Final-walk certification for landlords and incoming tenants.

  • Full-property decommission cleaning
  • Electrostatic fog disinfection (EPA-registered)
  • HVAC system disinfection
  • Mold remediation prep & surface treatment
  • Biological hazard area treatment
  • Furniture, fixture, and carpet haul-off coordination
  • Documentation for landlord and incoming tenant
Explore Property Decommission →
