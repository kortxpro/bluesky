Who We Serve

Six industries. One standard of care.

Every building type has its own failure modes, compliance demands, and occupancy pressures. Our programs are tuned to the context, with the same measurable discipline across every environment.

Corporate Offices Medical & Healthcare Retail & Commercial Education Construction Property Management
Industry 01

Corporate Offices

Class A and Class B environments, lobbies, executive floors, conference rooms, and amenity spaces, cleaned to the standards that occupants, not visitors, notice. Quiet operation. Low-disruption scheduling. Monthly performance reporting.

Typical programs

  • Nightly janitorial
  • Restroom disinfection
  • Common-area deep cleans (quarterly)
  • Glass and fingerprint management
  • Carpet care (quarterly)

What we solve

  • Inconsistent nightly quality
  • Missing or late detail cleans
  • Lack of written reporting
  • Safety and access compliance
Industry 02

Medical & Healthcare

Medical offices, outpatient clinics, dental, and veterinary facilities, disinfection protocols matched to practice type, EPA-registered products, and documentation that respects the compliance rigor patient environments demand.

Typical programs

  • Clinical-grade disinfection
  • Waiting room and exam room turns
  • Restroom, break room, and lab cleaning
  • Floor care (VCT / LVT / sealed concrete)
  • Trash & regulated-waste segregation

What we respect

  • HIPAA-adjacent discretion
  • OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards
  • EPA-registered disinfectant dwell times
  • After-hours access and key control
Industry 03

Retail & Commercial

Shopping centers, storefronts, restaurants, and service commerce, scheduled around customer traffic, never through it. Pressure-washing on open-to-public schedules, floor care that respects merchandise, and restrooms maintained to brand standard.

Typical programs

  • Storefront interior cleaning
  • Common area and food court programs
  • Exterior pressure washing
  • Window cleaning (monthly / quarterly)
  • Restroom deep cycles

Operational fit

  • Overnight / pre-open shifts
  • Holiday and peak-season surge crews
  • Multi-location consistency
  • Consolidated invoicing
Industry 04

Education

Private schools, colleges, and training centers, standards-driven cleaning that protects the environment students and staff share. Green-certified products where the institution requires them, written protocols, and vetted, background-checked teams.

Typical programs

  • Classroom & office nightly cleaning
  • Gym, cafeteria, restroom programs
  • Summer / holiday deep resets
  • Floor refinishing on breaks
  • Event post-clean support

What we provide

  • Background-checked personnel
  • Green-seal aligned product options
  • Documented sanitation frequencies
  • Supervisor walk-through logs
Industry 05

Construction

General contractors, developers, and construction managers, rough, final, and turnover cleans that get certificates of occupancy signed on time. Equipped for new-build, renovation, and disaster-related rebuild work.

Typical scopes

  • Rough clean (post-drywall / trades)
  • Final clean (pre-turnover)
  • Punch-list cleans
  • Post-construction glass & sealant
  • Rebuild / restoration support

How we operate

  • Coordinated with project schedule
  • PPE and safety orientation
  • Debris staging and haul-off
  • Certificate-of-occupancy turnover quality
Industry 06

Property Management

Multi-tenant buildings, mixed-use portfolios, and HOAs, one partner, multiple properties, consolidated reporting. Property managers get a single point of contact, standardized service levels, and monthly performance briefs per building.

Portfolio services

  • Common-area and amenity cleaning
  • Exterior programs (pressure / windows)
  • Unit-turn and make-ready cleaning
  • Emergency response & on-call
  • Monthly QA inspections

For the portfolio

  • Single account manager
  • Consolidated invoicing
  • Standardized service-level agreements
  • Multi-property reporting dashboard

Tell us about your portfolio.

One building, one portfolio, or an emergency event, we will respond with a scoped proposal within three business days.
