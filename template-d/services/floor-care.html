Service 05

Floor Stripping & Waxing

VCT, terrazzo, concrete, and LVT restoration, strip, scrub, seal, and finish, scheduled around tenant occupancy, not through it. Documented process, sealer specifications, and dry times. Multi-coat protocols.

What's Included

The floor-care scope.

Surface-specific process, strip, clean, seal, finish, with dry times and cure schedules respected to protect the finish.

  • VCT strip & wax (multi-coat finish)
  • Terrazzo polish & diamond grind
  • Concrete densification & seal
  • LVT deep clean & protective coat
  • Epoxy / polished-concrete maintenance
  • Burnishing on rotating schedule
  • Spray-buff and high-speed restoration
  • Baseboard & grout detail
  • Slip-resistance treatments where required
  • Occupancy-aware scheduling

Our Process

Four steps from inquiry to active program.

Every engagement follows the same documented process, so you know what we are doing, and when.

Site walk & floor review.

We review floor type, existing finish condition, wear patterns, access timing, and whether the scope is restoration, maintenance, or a full strip-and-wax reset.

Scoped proposal.

Delivered within three business days, with finish system, coat count, dry times, occupancy notes, and the operational plan tied to the site.

Mobilization.

Crews, machines, chemistry, and sequencing are coordinated so the floor can be restored without colliding with building operations.

Active reporting.

You receive visibility into completed work, finish status, cure windows, and the cadence needed to keep the floor presentation at standard.
