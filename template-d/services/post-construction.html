Service 06

Post-Construction

Rough clean, final clean, punch-list support, and turnover-ready detailing for commercial projects. When a site shifts from normal closeout into emergency response, our disaster recovery capability extends the same operational discipline into a much harder environment.

What's Included

The post-construction scope.

From rough clean through certificate of occupancy, with documented turnover support and recovery coordination when the site condition goes beyond normal closeout.

  • Rough clean (post-drywall / trades)
  • Final clean (pre-turnover)
  • Punch-list cleans
  • Post-construction glass & sealant removal
  • Debris removal & haul-off
  • Surface detail for handoff and owner walk-throughs
  • Phased-area reopening support
  • Recovery escalation planning when damage is present
  • Documentation for owner, GC, and property teams
Our Process

Four steps from inquiry to active program.

Every engagement follows the same documented process, so you know what we are doing, and when.

Site walk & scope.

We walk the facility, measure what drives cost (square footage, traffic, fixtures, occupancy hours), and draft a written scope.

Scoped proposal.

Delivered within three business days, pricing, schedule, inspection framework, reporting cadence, and escalation paths.

Mobilization.

Teams are vetted, trained on your specific scope, and onboarded with supervisor oversight for the first full cycle.

Active reporting.

Monthly performance brief, inspection scores, completed extras, incidents, response times. You see what we see.
