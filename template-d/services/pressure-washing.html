Service 02

Pressure Washing

Exterior surfaces restored on schedule, facades, parking structures, sidewalks, dumpster pads, and drive-throughs. Surface-appropriate PSI, proper degreasers, and water recovery where required. Documented technique. Safety program per OSHA.

What's Included

The pressure-washing scope.

Building envelopes, horizontal surfaces, back-of-house, and specialty cleans, each with its own PSI and chemistry.

  • Facade & building-envelope soft wash
  • Concrete, pavers, and sidewalk deep clean
  • Parking structure degreasing
  • Drive-through and dumpster-pad maintenance
  • Storefront entryway cleaning
  • Graffiti and gum removal
  • Awning and signage rinse-down
  • Water reclamation where regulated
  • Surface-appropriate PSI selection
  • Post-service documentation photos

Our Process

Four steps from inquiry to active program.

Every engagement follows the same documented process, so you know what we are doing, and when.

Site walk & scope.

We walk the facility, measure what drives cost (square footage, traffic, fixtures, occupancy hours), and draft a written scope.

Scoped proposal.

Delivered within three business days, pricing, schedule, inspection framework, reporting cadence, and escalation paths.

Mobilization.

Teams are vetted, trained on your specific scope, and onboarded with supervisor oversight for the first full cycle.

Active reporting.

Monthly performance brief, inspection scores, completed extras, incidents, response times. You see what we see.
