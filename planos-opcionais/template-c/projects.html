Hurricane Milton Recovery

Disaster Recovery
30+ Team Members
24/7 Emergency Response
Full Building Restored
Multi-Floor Commercial High-Rise

When Hurricane Milton struck, a crane collapsed into a commercial high-rise, causing catastrophic damage across multiple floors. Water flooded the building, carpets were destroyed, ceilings collapsed, and debris covered every surface.

BlueSky General Services deployed a 30-person crew within hours. The team worked around the clock, extracting water, removing thousands of square feet of damaged carpet, deploying industrial dehumidifiers across every floor, and restoring the building's premium lobby and common areas to full operation.

The scope of work included complete debris removal, water extraction, floor preparation, industrial drying, and final detailing of all common areas, offices, and corridors.

Scope of work

  • Emergency water extraction
  • Damaged carpet and flooring removal
  • Industrial dehumidifier deployment
  • Debris removal and hauling
  • Ceiling and wall debris cleanup
  • Lobby and common area restoration
  • Office and corridor detailing
  • Final inspection and handover
Featured in local news — BlueSky's Hurricane Milton response was covered by local television media.
